Lord Rama, The Ideal Man

Source: Ramayana Family: Son of Dasharatha and Kaushalya

Biography

Lord Rama is the seventh avatar of the Hindu god Vishnu and the central figure of the Ramayana. He is considered the ideal man (Maryada Purushottam) and exemplifies righteousness, duty, honor, and compassion.

Key Stories

  • Birth and childhood in Ayodhya
  • Marriage to Sita by breaking Shiva's bow
  • 14-year exile to the forest
  • Abduction of Sita by Ravana
  • Building alliance with Sugriva and Hanuman
  • Battle of Lanka and defeat of Ravana
  • Return to Ayodhya and coronation
